Section 01 · Overview

The fastest route to positive EBITDA runs through the bottom half of the existing network, not through new openings

Code Ninjas grew FY2025 revenue 10.7% to $11.354m while operating EBITDA fell to −$518k and year-end cash was $68,560. The centers that already exist hold the answer: about 45% of reporting Learning Centers sit below our illustrative fully staffed break-even. Retention, price yield, procurement and HQ discipline are worth more than unit growth, and the illustrative plan in this packet moves EBITDA to roughly +$0.38m within 18 to 24 months.
